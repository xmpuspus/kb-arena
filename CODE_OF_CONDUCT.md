# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces professional, specific, and respectful.
Examples of acceptable participation include:

- giving actionable technical feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting errors;
- respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints;
- focusing disagreements on evidence and project outcomes.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, publication of
private information, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, project events,
and public communication made on behalf of KB Arena.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `xavier@xmpuspus.dev`. Include links or records that help the
maintainer understand the event. The maintainer may remove content, issue a warning, limit access,
or ban participation based on the impact and pattern of conduct.

The project will protect reporter privacy as far as practical. It cannot promise confidentiality
when legal or safety duties apply.
